Do Saal Ka Safar

2 Year Anniversary Poem Dedicated to Nivedita
Playing Poem Audio

Do saal beet gaye, pata hi nahi chala,
Jaise andheri raaton mein koi chand khila.
Tere hone se meri har subah hai haseen,
Tere jaisa is duniya mein aur koi nahi.


Woh pehli mulaqaat, woh baatein hazaar,
Dil ke kone mein bas gaya tera pyaar.
Kabhi roothna tera, kabhi manana mera,
Is chote se safar mein bas gaya tera basera.


Haath mein haath lekar yunhi chalte rehna,
Bina kuch kahe sab kuch keh dena.
Khuda se bas yahi ek duaa maangte hain,
Har janam mein tujhko hi apna maante hain.


Happy 2nd Anniversary, meri jaan, meri Nivedita! ❤️
